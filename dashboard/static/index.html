Performance · Cost · Responsibility

Every model response, judged before it reaches the user.

ControlPlane.ai is a reverse proxy between an application and its LLM provider. It intercepts responses at sentence granularity, scores them across three planes, and routes each one through a policy decision — pass, edit, block, or escalate. The policy is configuration, not code.

The thesis

One response. Three policies. Three different verdicts.

The same assistant sentence containing a customer's SSN is edited under the support-bot policy, blocked under the HR-copilot policy, and escalated to a human under the finance-advisor policy. Nothing about the detectors changes between those three runs — only the YAML.

EDIT

support_bot — redact the span, deliver the rest.

BLOCK

hr_copilot — withhold and substitute the fallback.

ESCALATE

finance_advisor — quarantine for human review.

Reproduce: python -m pytest tests/test_gateway_app.py -k beat4 · dataset case PII-001 (frozen)

What ships today

Six of eleven specified detectors are live and measured. The list below is what runs — not what is planned.

Sentence-granular interception

Streaming responses are checked at each sentence boundary, so a breach is caught mid-stream rather than after delivery. Non-streaming pipelines buffer fully and take one verdict.

Tier-1 pattern detectors

PII and blocklist matching at P99 0.133 ms and 0.020 ms against 2 ms budgets. Precision 1.000 on PII — it does not over-fire.

Model-backed detectors

Prompt-injection and toxicity classifiers plus RAG grounding and NER enrichment, quantised to int8 and run on CPU on a dedicated single-worker pool.

Policy engine, four actions

Multi-label signals converge by documented severity order into pass / edit / block / escalate. Agreement with the specification: 1.000 over 280 cases × 3 policies.

Fail-open / fail-closed per policy

When a detector times out, the configured failure mode decides — not the runner. The identical fault passes under two policies and escalates under the third.

Append-only audit + human review

Every request writes one record with signals, verdict, coverage and latency. Escalations land in a review queue. The category of a PII hit is logged, never the value.

Not shipped — on the roadmap

Recorded as standing limitations in docs/08-open-questions.md rather than left implied.

  • SL-6Self-consistency scorer — specified, unimplemented. The UC-3 performance plane is covered by RAG grounding instead.
  • SL-7Calibrated thresholds — the conformal band inverts on the frozen corpus at every α tried, so no calibrated τ ships. Thresholds remain pre-calibration seeds.
  • SL-8Injection detector tail latency — P99 25.348 ms against a 25 ms budget. Published as a breach; the budget was not moved.
  • roadmapCost plane, conversation tracker, deep audit lane — semantic entropy and fairness auditing are specified as an async lane and are not built.
